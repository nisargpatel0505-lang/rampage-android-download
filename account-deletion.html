Request deletion by email

Email nisargpatel0505@gmail.com with the subject RAMPAGE account deletion request. Where possible, send it from the email you used to sign in to RAMPAGE. You can copy this address into any email service if the link does not open your mail app.

The developer may ask you to verify ownership before deleting an account and will confirm the outcome by email. A request is not completed merely by opening this page. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens or sign-in codes.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes the Firebase Authentication identity used by RAMPAGE, including its associated email, display name, provider profile information and Firebase user ID. This version has no cloud campaign data. Firebase states that authentication data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after deletion is initiated; authentication IP logs are retained for a few weeks. See Firebase’s retention information.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, upgrades, mission history, tutorial progress and settings are saved locally and are independent of the account. To erase them, use Android Settings → Apps → RAMPAGE → Storage → Clear storage. This permanently resets local progress on that device. Review your Android backup settings separately. Deleting RAMPAGE’s identity does not delete your Google account.

From the game

You can also open the signed-in account button on the RAMPAGE home screen, select Delete account, and confirm. An internet connection and a recent sign-in may be required.
