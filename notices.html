Original game assets

The city maps, characters, animations, portraits and game effects were created for RAMPAGE. The models were generated using Blender. No PUBG or Mob Control models, textures, animation files, music or sound effects are included.

The original synthesized sound effects and City Afterglow music are available under the original audio notice / CC0 dedication. Blender and the glTF exporter/validator are development tools; their executables are not game assets.
