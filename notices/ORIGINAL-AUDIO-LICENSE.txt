RAMPAGE original procedural audio

These WAV files were created specifically for this project from mathematical
oscillators, envelopes and seeded noise. No recorded performances, downloaded
samples, game music, PUBG audio or Mob Control audio were used.

Composition: City Afterglow, original 64-second instrumental loop, 120 BPM.
Generation source: Scripts/Audio/Tools~/generate_audio.py
The NumPy dependency is used only offline to generate assets, not shipped in the app.
